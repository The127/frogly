# frogly design demo

Static pages that show how frogly could look. The
[vision](../vision.md) explains the words they use, like garden, harvest
and compost.

Open `today.html` in a browser. Use "next" to go through today, the
garden, the board, the compost and the weekly digest. Click an idea or a
ticket to open it. "switch to dark" and "switch to light" change the
theme.

The pages are a sketch, not a spec. The people, projects and numbers in
them are made up.
